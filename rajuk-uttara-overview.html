RAJUK Uttara Apartment Project (Sector 18)

The RAJUK Uttara Apartment Project (RUAP) in Sector 18, Uttara Model Town (3rd Phase) is Bangladesh's premier government-developed residential township. Spanning 214 acres, it offers planned modern living, green open spaces, and world-class public infrastructure.

1. Master Plan & Key Specifications

Total Area 214 Acres
Location Sector 18, Uttara Phase 3, Dhaka
Total Towers (Block A) 79 Sixteen-Story (16-Story) Buildings
Standard Unit Gross Area 1,650 Gross Sq Ft (approx 1,270 Net Sq Ft)

2. Block A: Ready Apartments for Resale & Handover

Block A consists of fully constructed, earthquake-resistant 16-story towers equipped with modern high-speed lifts, dedicated sub-stations, and 24/7 security.

Typical Apartment Layout (1,650 sq ft):

    3 Spacious Bedrooms 3 Bathrooms (Attaches & Common) 3 Private Verandahs/Balconies Living Room, Dining Space, & Kitchen

3. Block B & Block C: Future Development & Investment

While Block A is handed over and operational, Block B and Block C represent future allotment phases under RAJUK's expansion roadmap. These blocks are planned for mid-income citizens, government employees, and NRB (Non-Resident Bangladeshi) applicants. Buying or securing rights in these sectors offers long-term capital appreciation as Phase 3 infrastructure matures.

4. Strategic Connectivity & Metro Rail (MRT Line 6)

One of the biggest advantages of RAJUK Uttara Sector 18 is its direct proximity to Dhaka’s Metro Rail infrastructure:

  • 🔹 Uttara Center Metro Station: Located less than 1.5 km away, offering effortless commute to Farmgate, Motijheel, and Agargaon.
  • 🔹 Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport: Approx. 15-20 minutes via Dhaka-Mymensingh Highway or Elevated Expressway.
  • 🔹 Internal Roads: Wide 60ft to 100ft wide internal avenues with underground utilities.

5. Pricing Breakdown, Resale Fees & Loan Options

Resale flat prices in RAJUK Uttara Sector 18 vary depending on floor location, tower orientation, corner units, and interior fit-out condition.

Resale Price Range

Depending on floor level and orientation, ready flats range from BDT 7,500,000 to BDT 9,500,000+ (excluding official RAJUK transfer fees).

Bank Loan Eligibility

Eligible for up to 70% Home Loan financing from DBH, Mutual Trust Bank, City Bank, and IDLC due to 100% clear government land title.
